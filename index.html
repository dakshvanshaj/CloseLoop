    The problem this solves

    When a deal is marked as won, that's where the sales side's job ends and the ops side's job should start — but most of the time, nothing tells ops the deal closed. The handoff depends on someone remembering to mention it, which means:

    • Ops finds out about a new deal only when something goes wrong — a client calls asking where their onboarding is.
    • Friday-afternoon closes sit untouched until Monday, because nobody's watching for them over the weekend.

    CloseLoop runs that handoff automatically: the moment a deal closes, the right person is told, and a quick weekly recap catches anything that still slipped through. There's no new app for anyone to learn — it talks to the tools your team already uses.
